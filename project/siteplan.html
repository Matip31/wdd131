Site Plan

Site Name

MateMatiCrazy

I chose this name because my name is Matias, and people call me Mati. It plays with the spanish word "Matematica". Also, people that like mate tend to be called "Locos por el mate" or Crazy for the Mate, so it is a word game with those words. This is a mate store designed for Mate fans, therefore the name fits just fine and is easy to remember.

Site Purpose

This website has the purpose of showcasing the products from a Mate Store, let the user pick their favorite ones, and then contact the store to know more about them and potentially buy them.

Scenarios

  • What are the best Mate products that people are interested in buying?
  • How much variety of products do I need to include?

Color Schema

Blue #5DADE2

Used for header, navigation bar, and section headings

Green #4CAF50

Used for buttons, calls to action, and mate related graphics.

Dark Green #2E7D32

Used for button hover effects and secondary accents

Yellow #F4D03F

Used for highlights, icons, and special accents throughout the site

White #FFFFFF

Used as the primary background color to keep the design clean and easy to read
